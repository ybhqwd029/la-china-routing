## The Holy Trinity of China Routing — CN2 GIA, CUII/9929, and CMIN2

Before we talk about specific plans, let's get the terminology straight. Because the hosting world loves throwing around acronyms like they're secret handshake passwords.

**CN2 GIA (Global Internet Access)** is China Telecom's premium backbone. Think of it as the express lane on the China-bound highway. Regular CN2 (sometimes called CN2 GT) also exists, but GIA is the top tier — it has higher priority, less congestion, and routes directly to China Telecom end users. If your primary audience is on China Telecom (which, statistically, a big chunk of China is), this is the line you want.

**CUII/AS9929** is China Unicom's premium backbone. Same idea, different carrier. Unicom users connect through this instead of CN2 GIA.

**CMIN2/AS58807** is China Mobile's premium international network. Same concept, third carrier.

The best "West Coast CN2 GIA VPS" setups don't just offer one of these — they offer all three, routing each Chinese carrier's users through their own premium backbone. That's called "tri-network optimization" (三网优化), and it's what separates a genuinely good China-optimized VPS from one that just slaps "CN2" on the product page and calls it a day.

ZgoCloud's Los Angeles AMD Optimised VPS line does exactly this: GIA + 9929 + CMIN2, all in one plan. Telecom users hit CN2 GIA, Unicom users hit 9929, Mobile users hit CMIN2. No one gets shoved onto a congested transit link.
